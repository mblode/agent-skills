## Professional but friendly, no marketing hype

Documentation is not a sales pitch. Readers arrive with a task to complete, not a purchase to justify. Superlatives ("blazing-fast," "revolutionary," "world-class") erode trust because they promise without proving. Marketing language also ages poorly as competitors catch up.

Be specific instead. Replace subjective claims with measurable facts. A friendly, confident tone comes from clarity, not enthusiasm.

**Incorrect (marketing language and superlatives):**

```markdown
Our revolutionary, industry-leading API delivers blazing-fast
performance that will supercharge your workflow. With our incredibly
powerful SDK, you'll experience unparalleled ease of integration
that simply blows the competition away.
```

**Correct (specific, helpful, and direct):**

```markdown
The API processes requests in under 50ms for most operations. The SDK
handles authentication, retries, and pagination so you can focus on
your application logic. See the [benchmarks](benchmarks.md) for
detailed performance data.
```

Reference: [Microsoft Style Guide — Bias-free communication](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/style-guide/bias-free-communication)

How we count a delivery

Written by Tomas Varga on 21 October 2026

For three years a delivery was counted when the driver tapped Done. That was simple, and it was wrong about one stop in forty.

What the old number hid

A tap is a claim, not a handover. When we compared taps against signed receipts, the gap was largest on multi-drop routes, where a driver clears several stops in one go at the end of the street.

We were paying bonuses on a number the customer never saw.

What we count now

A delivery now counts when there is a signature, a photo, or a safe-place code. All three are timestamped on the device, so a van with no signal still reports in the right order.

The change lowered our headline number by 2.4% and cut disputed invoices by a third.

Changes to Harborline pricing from January

We are changing our plans on 12 January 2027. This post covers what changes, why, and what you need to do. For most teams the answer to the last part is nothing.

What is changing

We are moving from two plans to three, and every plan now includes the shared inbox, which used to be a paid add-on at $6 per seat.

PlanPrice per seat, per monthSeatsScheduling history
Starter$12Up to 1090 days
Team$29Up to 1002 years
Fleet$79UnlimitedUnlimited

Annual billing still takes two months off the yearly total on every plan.

Why we are doing this

The old Growth plan tried to serve a six-person depot and a 400-driver fleet with one price. Small teams paid for audit logs they never opened, and large teams hit the 50-seat ceiling and had to call us to keep working.

Splitting the plan lets us drop the entry price from $15 to $12 and stop gating the shared inbox, which 71% of accounts were already paying extra for.

What stays the same

  • The route planner, driver app, and proof-of-delivery photos are in every plan.
  • There is still no charge for dispatcher seats that are read-only.
  • Your data export works the same way and stays free.

What you need to do

If you are on Growth with 10 seats or fewer, you move to Starter on your next renewal and your bill goes down. If you have more than 10 seats, you move to Team.

You can switch plans yourself under Settings, then Billing, or write to billing@harborline.example and we will do it with you.

Existing customers keep their current prices until 31 March 2027, and nobody is moved to a more expensive plan before that date without agreeing to it first.

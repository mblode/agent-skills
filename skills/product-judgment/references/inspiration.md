# Inspiration and Boundaries

The method adapts product practices, not a company's entire operating model. External writing is evidence about its author; the consuming project's actual choices supply the product constraints.

## Linear

- [Karri Saarinen's linear-way](https://gist.github.com/ksaa/5644e3225be8af5eef4fa1bed0e7e88a): reframe requests as needs, examine inaction, compare alternatives, and use product context. This is an original source prompt, not a command to act as a Linear employee.
- [How Linear designs with AI](https://stateofaidesign.com/cases/linear): use AI to recover historical and customer context before committing to a solution. A prompt cannot substitute for access to that context.
- [Output isn't design](https://linear.app/now/output-isn-t-design): creating form does not establish that the problem is understood. Exploration can include prototypes; do not turn this into a ban on thinking through making.
- [The Linear Method](https://linear.app/method): direction, useful goals, scope, momentum, and building with users. Cadence and issue formats are contextual, not prerequisites here.
- [Starting Linear](https://karrisaarinen.com/posts/starting-linear-app/): a product direction rooted in experienced workflow friction. Personal frustration starts a hypothesis; it does not prove broad demand.
- [Why is quality so rare?](https://linear.app/now/why-is-quality-so-rare) and [Conversations on Quality](https://linear.app/quality): quality belongs to the whole experience. The latter is an interview-series index, not a single methodology.

[Karri Saarinen, Inside Linear: Building with taste, craft, and focus](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/inside-linear-building-with-taste), October 8, 2023. Relevant sections: craft at 06:43, product management at 16:41, design reviews at 33:08, and customer focus at 48:04.

[Nan Yu, Linear's secret to building beloved B2B products](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/linears-secret-to-building-beloved-b2b-products-nan-yu), January 30, 2025. The publisher's takeaways support resisting customization that worsens core workflows, exploring extreme alternatives, and cutting scope around meaningful deadlines. Relevant sections: bloat at 15:31, needs at 23:57, creativity at 44:46, and deadlines at 01:09:15.

These notes use the publisher's episode pages and takeaways, not a claim to have reviewed every spoken exchange. Neither episode justifies a universal ban on metrics, product managers, or experiments.

## Vercel and Done Bear

The Vercel brand-engineering example in the sibling `branding` skill reinforces making repeated work easier after the desired experience is understood. It does not make Vercel's appearance a product-quality criterion.

The author's Done Bear project supplied concrete examples of doctrine maps, standing refusals, and asking whether a need is already served. Generalize the method, not Done Bear's positioning, category restrictions, unavailable feedback sources, or decision taxonomy. This portable skill permits proceed and investigate as well as decline; a high rejection rate is not evidence of good judgment.

# LinkedIn voice

Built from 12 posts.

## Measured

- 40 to 90 words in 2 to 4 short blocks.
- Opens with what shipped or what happened. No hook line.
- Credits the team by name when the names are known.
- No hashtags, no emoji.

## Redacted excerpts

- "We turned off the old billing service on Friday. It ran for 6 years.\n\nThanks to (names) for 3 months of careful work."
- "Hiring a second platform engineer in Melbourne. Details in the comments."

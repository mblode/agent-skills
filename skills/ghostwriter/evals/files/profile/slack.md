# Slack voice

Synthetic fixture, 40 messages.

## Measured

- Median message 48 characters; 80% under 120; longest 260.
- Opens with the answer 70%, "Yep" 20%, a question back 10%. Never "Hey team".
- One paragraph almost always; no bullets under 4 items; no headings.
- Emoji in 1 of 12 messages, always at the end. Never on a bug or a delay.
- Lowercase start in about half of messages.

## Not me

- "Great question! Happy to help with this one 🙌" (cheerleading, emoji up front)
- "Quick update on the migration: we've made significant progress and are on track." (vague, no number)

# Blog voice

Built from 14 posts.

## Measured

- Posts run 150 to 600 words; a post with one thing to say stays under 150.
- The first sentence is the thing that happened, with its number.
- Paragraphs of 1 to 3 sentences. No lists. Headings are rare, and noun phrases when used.
- First person, past tense. "We" for team work.

## Not me

- "In this post, I'll walk you through what I learned." (announces instead of starting)

## Redacted excerpts

- "The export job took 9 minutes on Monday. By Friday it took 40 seconds. Most of that was one index."
- "I reckon the old queue was fine. We outgrew it, that's all."

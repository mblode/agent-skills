# Inspiration and Boundaries

These are sources behind the approach, not policies that override the consuming brand. The synthesis is opinionated about coherence and execution; it prescribes no universal aesthetic.

## Vercel: expression that can be produced repeatedly

[Daniel Linthwaite, Brand Engineering, Design Engineering Night 8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xH1pRpkd9V0), published by Granola. Reviewed through English automatic captions; this is a paraphrase, not a transcript.

His examples connect concept exploration to production tooling: flexible templates, brand controls, and cross-format work. Transfer the distinction between exploratory generation and repeatable production. Do not import particular models, volumes, tools, or approval arrangements as requirements.

## Linear: judgment behind the form

[Output isn't design](https://linear.app/now/output-isn-t-design) grounds the distinction between an appealing output and a solved problem. [Why is quality so rare?](https://linear.app/now/why-is-quality-so-rare) connects care for the experience to business differentiation.

Transfer the connection between promise and experience. Do not infer that every brand should be restrained, monochrome, or aimed at software teams.

## Done Bear: a position has consequences

The author's Done Bear project informed the emphasis on product truth, meaningful refusals, and recognizable positioning. Its local product-judgment skill and doctrine map distinguish owned decisions from saved outside essays.

Transfer that distinction. Do not copy Done Bear's category exclusions, audience, tone, or local file paths into another brand. No access to Done Bear is required to use this skill.

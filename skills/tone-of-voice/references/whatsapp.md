# WhatsApp voice (demo persona)

Fictional demo persona: "Sam", a Melbourne-based senior engineer and indie builder. This file ships so the skill works out of the box. To make it yours, create `~/.config/tone-of-voice/whatsapp.md` from this structure and replace the rules and excerpts with your own (see `references/refreshing.md`). Everything below is invented, not real messages.

## Register

The most casual surface. Messages are short, plain, and immediate: reactions, plans, jokes, links. Warmth comes from doubled exclamation marks and ALL CAPS celebrations rather than emoji. The same person as Slack DMs, one notch looser, and with family it drops all work-speak entirely.

## Context splits

**Family** (parents, siblings, in-laws): plain logistics and affection. "Hahaha", "Smart!!", quick plan-making, life updates in 2-3 sentences. Big news given straight, softened with "haha" or 😅 rather than drama. Never quote or imitate the most private 1:1 relationships (e.g. a partner); that context is out of bounds for drafting.

**Close friends**: banter, plans ("Do you want to go climbing this weekend?"), running jokes, zero preamble. Reactions can be a single word: "Nah", "Sick", "Legend", "No way".

**Builder contacts** (founders, indie community): like Slack DMs but looser. Pitches projects in two short paragraphs with a link and a direct ask for thoughts. Schedules with the same "Easy," / booking-link moves as email.

**Product feedback to friends**: blunt, specific, design-literate nitpicks delivered kindly: "The site looks good! Just some nitpicky bits", then a burst of one-issue-per-message critiques.

**Group chats**: occasional drops, usually a link with a one-line take, or a single reaction to keep up with the room.

## Message shapes and length

- Typical message: under 10 words.
- Bursts are the norm: one thought per message, three messages in a row rather than one paragraph.
- Life updates max out around 3 sentences.
- Pitches/asks to builder contacts: 2 short paragraphs, link included, ends with the ask.

## Openers and closers

- "Hey," then the thing. "Yo," for closest friends. "Hey [name]," occasionally for family elders ("Hey dad,").
- Plans are proposed as direct questions: "Yo, do you want to grab lunch or hang this weekend?"
- No sign-offs, ever.
- Wrap-ups: "Easy, speak then!", "Enjoy!!", "See you Thursday".

## Emoji and punctuation

- Less emoji than you'd expect; "!!" does the emotional work.
- When used: 😅 (self-deprecating news), 🙏 (thanks/respect), 🎉 (celebration), 😮 (surprise), 👍 (ack).
- ALL CAPS multi-exclamation for big moments: "LETS GO!!!! 🎉 HUGE!!!!", "WOW!!".
- "Haha" prefix to disagree or tease gently: "Haha what's the other option?", "Hahaha you took 40 min to reply I didn't know you were in there".
- Phone curly apostrophes and the odd missing apostrophe both appear; do not over-polish.

## Anti-patterns

- Paragraph-length messages to friends or family.
- Work jargon or LinkedIn energy in family chats.
- Emoji strings (🎉🎉🥳🙌) in place of words.
- Greeting + name + sign-off formality.
- Em dashes.

## Excerpts

**Friends: making plans**
> Yo, do you want to grab lunch or hang this weekend?

> Do you want to go climbing this weekend?

> We're doing dinner at 6 but maybe we can do something tomorrow?

**Friends: banter**
> Hahaha you took 40 min to reply I didn't know you were in there

> Haha that would be amazing

**Family: logistics and reactions**
> Hey dad, keen to sit in on that call with [Sibling] and the accountant if it's still on

> Weirdly the invite didn't come through but we're meeting at 11:30 today

> Smart!!

**Family: news delivered straight, softened with 😅**
> We're staying put for now 😅 the house we liked went way over budget

**Family: life update (to a sibling)**
> I'm good! Just doing a bit of painting around the flat this weekend so it's chaos here

**Builder contact: pitching a project**
> Hey, I've been building a little thing: [link]

> It's a habit tracker that only asks you one question a day and does the rest from your calendar. Trying to see if the "less input" angle actually sticks

> Very keen to get your thoughts on it

**Builder contact: scheduling**
> Amazing!! Yeah grab a time here cal.com/sam-example

> Happy to chat tomorrow if you want and keen to see what you've built

> Hey, any chance we can push the call back an hour tomorrow or whenever suits

> Easy, moved it to 12 tomorrow

**Product feedback to a friend (burst of nitpicks)**
> The site looks good! Just some nitpicky bits

> The hero countdown is a bit hard to read broken into those squares

> I reckon the testimonials should be a swipeable carousel

> And strip the em dashes from the SEO titles, they read as AI-written and people skip them

**Big celebration**
> LETS GO!!!! 🎉 HUGE!!!!

**Group chat: link with a one-line take**
> Finally shipped [link to product news]

> Downloading the beta now

Ember Atlas shows wildfire risk on a 250 metre grid. Each cell carries a risk score from 0 to 100 and one of five bands.

## How often it updates

Weather inputs refresh every three hours. Fuel moisture refreshes daily at 06:00 UTC. Vegetation and slope are refreshed each quarter.

## Layers

- Risk score
- Fuel moisture
- Wind speed and direction at 10 metres
- Active incidents from the national feed

The risk score combines four inputs: fuel moisture, wind speed, slope, and vegetation type. Each input is scaled to 0 to 1 and weighted.

## Bands

- Low: 0 to 19
- Moderate: 20 to 39
- High: 40 to 59
- Very high: 60 to 79
- Extreme: 80 to 100

## Reading a cell

A cell's score is the worst three-hour window in the next 24 hours, not the current value. A calm morning before a windy afternoon shows the afternoon's score.

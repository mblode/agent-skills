---
title: Rate limits
description: Request quotas, burst allowance, and how to back off
order: 5
---

Each workspace may send 50 requests a second with a burst of 200. Carrier throughput limits apply on top and differ by country.

A request over the limit gets a 429 with a Retry-After header in seconds. Back off for that long, then resume.

Higher limits are available on the Scale plan.

---
title: Quiet hours
description: Hold non-urgent messages overnight in the recipient's timezone
order: 4
---

Set quiet hours on a workspace to hold messages between two local times. The recipient's timezone is taken from the number's country and area code.

A message sent with priority set to urgent ignores quiet hours. One-time passcodes should always be urgent.

Held messages are released in the order they were accepted, at the end of the quiet window.

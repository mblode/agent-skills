Frost dates

Your last spring frost is the date after which a ground frost is unlikely. Tender plants go out a week or two after it.

Finding yours

Keep a minimum thermometer at ground level and log every night below one degree. After three springs you will have a better date than any regional map.

Planning around it

Count back from the date: tomatoes are sown eight weeks before, courgettes four, runner beans three. The sowing calendar does the counting for a mid May frost.

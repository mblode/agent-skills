# Sources

Where the guidance in this skill came from, kept for whoever revises it next.

- House style guide, third edition, sections 2 (voice) and 5 (length).
- Editorial review notes from the spring relaunch: the eleven posts that were rewritten and why.
- Reader survey, autumn: "posts are too long" was the most common free-text answer, which is where the 900 word target came from.
- The tells list was compiled from drafts rejected by editors over two quarters. The original spreadsheet is in the editorial shared drive under "Rejected drafts".
- Length split rule: agreed in the editorial meeting after the three-part migration series outperformed the single long version.
